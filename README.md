# Amazon Sales Analytics

Tableau dashboard project analyzing Amazon sales data to understand revenue performance, customer activity, order trends, product categories, brands, payment methods and order status.

## 📊 Project Overview

This project analyzes Amazon sales data across multiple years to identify important patterns in sales performance and customer transactions.

An interactive Tableau dashboard was developed to provide a consolidated view of key business metrics and allow users to explore sales performance across categories, countries, brands, payment methods and order statuses.

The dashboard covers data from **2020 to 2024** and provides insights into revenue, customers, orders, quantity and average order value.

## 🎯 Business Objective

The objective of this project is to analyze sales performance and customer transaction patterns through an interactive business intelligence dashboard.

The analysis focuses on:

- Revenue performance
- Customer and order activity
- Product category performance
- Geographic sales distribution
- Brand-level revenue
- Payment method performance
- Order status distribution
- Average order value
- Year-wise analysis

## 🔍 Analysis Performed

### 1. Revenue by Category

Revenue was analyzed across major product categories to compare category-level sales performance.

The dashboard includes categories such as:

- Electronics
- Sports & Outdoors
- Books
- Clothing
- Toys & Games
- Home & Kitchen

Electronics generated approximately **15.58M** in revenue, followed by Sports & Outdoors at approximately **15.35M**.

### 2. Country-wise Sales

A geographic view was created to understand the distribution of sales across countries.

The dashboard highlights significant revenue contributions from countries including:

- United States
- India
- Canada
- United Kingdom
- Australia

The United States represents the largest displayed country-level revenue contribution at approximately **64.31M**.

### 3. Revenue by Brand

Brand-level revenue was analyzed to compare performance across different brands.

The dashboard includes brands such as:

- Zenith
- FitLife
- HomeEase
- UrbanStyle
- Apex
- BrightLux
- KiddoFun
- ReadMore
- CoreTech
- NexPro

Zenith recorded approximately **9.24M** in revenue, while UrbanStyle recorded approximately **9.25M**.

### 4. Order Status Distribution

Order status was analyzed to understand the distribution of transactions across different order outcomes.

The dashboard shows:

- Delivered: 75%
- Shipped: 15%
- Pending: 3%
- Returned: 3%
- Cancelled: 3%

Delivered orders represent the largest share of transactions at **75%**.

### 5. Revenue by Payment Method

Revenue was compared across different payment methods.

The dashboard includes:

- Credit Card
- Debit Card
- UPI
- Amazon Pay
- Net Banking
- Cash on Delivery

Credit Card generated the highest revenue at approximately **32.12M**, followed by Debit Card at approximately **18.54M**.

### 6. Year-wise Analysis

A year filter allows the dashboard to analyze sales activity across:

- 2020
- 2021
- 2022
- 2023
- 2024

This provides the ability to examine the dashboard metrics across different years.

## 💡 Key Insights

- Total revenue reached approximately **91.83M** from **100K orders**.
- The platform served approximately **43K customers**.
- Total transaction quantity reached approximately **300.14K**.
- The average order value was approximately **918.26**.
- **Electronics** generated the highest category revenue at approximately **15.58M** among the displayed categories.
- The **United States** recorded the highest displayed country-level revenue at approximately **64.31M**.
- **Credit Card** generated the highest revenue among payment methods at approximately **32.12M**.
- **Delivered orders accounted for 75%** of total transactions.
- **Shipped orders accounted for 15%** of total transactions.
- Brand-level revenue was relatively close across the displayed brands, with values around 9M each.
